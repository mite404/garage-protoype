## Boundaries

An imported prospect has an explicit specialist owner before any opportunity exists. Existing
opportunity membership remains unchanged. Human review remains required for identity, enrollment,
evidence, suggested actions and commercial decisions.

Resend sending is disabled unless an operator separately authorizes an allowlisted test policy.
Unknown credentials, Forms authentication, approved source configuration and model policy stay
disabled. No shared migrations, deployment, external messages or paid workflows are authorized.
